Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Country Royale publishes short animated videos of country-ball physics battles to its own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. Our own publishing tool posts to these accounts. Country Royale Studio also lets anyone post our battle videos to their own TikTok account.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data from viewers. To choose which nations appear in future videos, our tool reads public comments left on our own posts and counts which countries are named. We store only the comment ID, the commenter's public username as shown by the platform, and the countries mentioned, to avoid counting the same person twice. We reply to comments on our own posts.

Platform access

We use the official Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok interfaces to upload our videos, read comments on our own posts and read basic statistics for our own posts. Access tokens are stored securely and are never shared.

Country Royale Studio (TikTok)

When you sign in to Country Royale Studio with TikTok, we receive your TikTok basic profile (display name and profile picture) and permission to post the videos you choose. Your sign-in is kept only in your browser for that session; we do not store it. When you press Post, we send the video you picked, with the caption, privacy and interaction settings you chose, to TikTok. We keep a record of which battle you posted, linked to the anonymous ID TikTok gives us, so the Studio can show it as posted. We never post anything you did not choose, and you can sign out at any time or remove Country Royale Studio from your TikTok settings under Security, Manage app permissions.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Deletion

To have a comment record or a Studio posting record removed, message us on any of our accounts and we will delete it within 30 days.
