Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Country Royale publishes short animated videos of country-ball physics battles to its own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. Our publishing tool is used only by Country Royale to post to these accounts.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data from viewers. To choose which nations appear in future videos, our tool reads public comments left on our own posts and counts which countries are named. We store only the comment ID, the commenter's public username as shown by the platform, and the countries mentioned, to avoid counting the same person twice. We reply to comments on our own posts.

Platform access

We use the official Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok interfaces to upload our videos, read comments on our own posts and read basic statistics for our own posts. Access tokens are stored securely and are never shared.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Deletion

To have a comment record removed, message us on any of our accounts and we will delete it within 30 days.

Contact

Message @countryroyale.arena on Instagram.
